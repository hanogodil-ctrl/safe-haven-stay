BE AWARE. BE PREPARED.

Safety Centre

Simple tips to help you make safer decisions.

🌙 Night Travel

• Plan your route before leaving.

• Keep your phone charged.

• Let someone you trust know your journey.

• Prefer well-lit and populated routes.

🚕 Taxi & Rickshaw Safety

• Check the vehicle and driver details.

• Confirm the destination before travelling.

• Share your trip details with a trusted person.

• If uncomfortable, move to a safe public place.

👁 If You Feel Followed

• Avoid going directly home.

• Move towards a busy, well-lit place.

• Contact someone you trust.

• Contact local emergency services if threatened.

📱 Digital Safety

• Keep your phone locked.

• Do not share your passwords.

• Only share location with trusted people.
